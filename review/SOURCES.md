## Scope

Official pages were read using web retrieval on the date above. This was a public-name, stated-activity, geography and link-purpose check. It was not independent verification of charitable registration, impact, financial performance, tax eligibility, fees or transaction completion. No payment, login, account creation or message was submitted. HTTP results are separately recorded in `link-checks.json`; retrieval failures are not automatically proof of broken links. Dates are hard-coded editorial records, not generated at render time.

## Other evidence and decisions

- Live routes returned meaningful HTML without client execution. Existing Next.js architecture already supports prerendering; no SSR migration was necessary.
- Live `/robots.txt` and `/sitemap.xml` returned 404; arbitrary invalid route also returned 404 (the latter is correct).
- Live homepage H1 measured 96px with 19.2px tracking at 1440px; live India document width measured 1204px at viewport 1180px.
- The live homepage says established 2025; the local copy says 2024. Both dates were omitted rather than choosing an unsupported one.
- No custom cursor implementation was found in the supplied source. Global motion, glow, rainbow text, decorative animations and hover transforms were removed.
- No correction contact or specific personal origin story exists in the supplied source. These were asked for during the redesign and remain unfilled unless supplied. Do not publish a fabricated address.
- The short founder introduction uses only the existing facts: Rignesh, student, creator, and the project’s stated exploration of celebrations and giving.
- Original disclaimer's no-processing and student-project substance is retained, with wording corrected to cover homepages and product destinations. The exact old source is in `original-source.tar.gz`.

### Browser follow-up

The retained WWF donation URL was subsequently opened in a normal browser. Its redirect successfully displayed an official appeal/giving-options landing page; the earlier retrieval challenge was resolved without bypassing a security warning. CRY returned a 307 challenge to the HTTP check but successfully displayed its official homepage in the normal browser. Neither was treated as a broken link. No final payment form was submitted. The WWF listing now reflects the successful landing-page inspection.
